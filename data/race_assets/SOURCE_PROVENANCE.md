# Provenance of supplementary visual snapshots

The Race Card inputs in this directory are curated candidate, polling, demographic, historical and finance snapshots supplied from the public-data compilation formerly distributed at https://github.com/ElectIndex/26_us_forecast_data. Underlying observations cite public election, Census, FEC and polling records, but their particular mapping and curation have not been rebuilt independently in this project. This provenance record does not indicate that the external publisher contributed to NUCLEUS 42’s forecasting methodology.

The data are **presentation-only**. They must not be confused with the unchanged, manually maintained v30 forecast workbook or entered as new predictors. Photograph-source and identity metadata are retained per record; rights for individual images must be checked separately.
