The data behind the race profiles

The validated national / House / Senate engines remain the statistical baseline. External demographic, fundraising and candidate-strength variables are research inputs until matched historical vintages, geographic crosswalks and out-of-cycle validation establish improvement. Provider forecast outputs and provider “strength” scores are not training observations.
