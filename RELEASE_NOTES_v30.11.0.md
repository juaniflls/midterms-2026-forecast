# Nucleus 42 v30.11.0

This release completes the v30 publication architecture for the 2026 U.S. midterm forecast.

## Highlights

- One race-first forecast across all 435 House districts and 35 scheduled Senate elections.
- A 12-state monitored Senate layer integrated with structural coverage of every scheduled race.
- 50,000 complete-election simulations with distinct point, probability and stability outputs.
- Autonomous publication HTML with maps, race desks, filters, CSV exports, image capture and Scenario Lab.
- Scenario Lab v30.7 with 31 controls, 14 intervention units, directed reconciliation, incumbent-aware interpretation and exact baseline identity.
- Native Render/Dash navigation retained: Overview, Explore House, Explore Senate, Probability, Simulation, Methodology, Validation and Scenario Lab.
- All-in-One serves the exact autonomous HTML; the native Scenario Lab consumes its canonical serialized contract.
- Updated branding, repository documentation, validation workflow and social-preview asset.
