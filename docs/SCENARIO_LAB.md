# NUCLEUS 42 · Scenario Lab · v27.1

Scenario Lab is the counterfactual layer of NUCLEUS 42. It asks how the same frozen forecast responds when selected national conditions change coherently.

It never overwrites the official forecast and it does not claim causal effects.

## Baseline identity
Reset returns:
- Popular vote: D52.07% · R45.01% · Other2.92%
- House: D230 · R205
- Senate: D50 · R50
- all 31 observed controls
- all 42 national targets

## Controls
- 31 national controls
- 14 intervention units
- 12 constrained composition batteries
- 2 independent macroeconomic controls

## Hard constraints
Battery totals cannot exceed 100%. Direct edits are preserved when feasible. Latest-edit priority resolves incompatible requests inside one battery. Compatible edits across different batteries persist.

## Learned reconciliation
Untouched controls are reconciled through regularized historical cross-unit relationships. Within-battery statistical influence is zero; battery movement comes from hard arithmetic.

## Execution
1. apply direct edits;
2. enforce hard constraints;
3. reconcile untouched controls;
4. build a coherent 31-control snapshot;
5. rerun 42 targets;
6. translate through 435 House districts;
7. translate through all 35 Senate elections.

## House
The visible scenario headline is the sum of the 435 district winners, not rounded expected seat mass.

## Senate
The 11 monitored races begin from the same coherent central tuple as the official forecast. The 24 Safe races use scenario-only structural sensitivity anchors and are not public numerical forecasts.

## Interpretation
Scenario Lab is associational, not causal. Extreme combinations may extrapolate beyond historical support.
